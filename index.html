About

I’m Gabby Louiee, a Kenyan singer, songwriter, poet, and actor based in Mombasa.

My work brings acoustic storytelling together with soulful vocals, guitar, and poetry to explore love, identity, and the human experience. On stage, I perform original music with a calm, expressive, emotionally grounded presence.
